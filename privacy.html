Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

This site is the personal portfolio of Abhay Chandra. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email aaryachandra41@gmail.com.

What is collected

The site only collects information you choose to send through the website application form, the one beneath the manhole. That form asks for your name, email address, company or brand, the kind of project, budget, timeline, and a description of the project.

There are no accounts, no tracking cookies, no advertising, and no analytics on this site.

How it is used

Your application is used only to read your enquiry and reply to you about your project. It is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Who handles it

When you press send, the form is delivered by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service, which forwards it to Abhay’s inbox. FormSubmit processes the data under its own privacy policy. Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers and shares your IP address with them, as described in Google’s privacy policy.

How long it is kept

Applications are kept in Abhay’s email for as long as needed to discuss and deliver your project, then deleted on request or when no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete anything you have sent at any time by emailing aaryachandra41@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, and those requests will be honoured.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
